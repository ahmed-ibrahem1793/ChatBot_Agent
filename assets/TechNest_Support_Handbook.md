# TechNest Customer Support Handbook

Internal reference for support agents and automated assistants. Covers shipping, returns, payments, accounts, troubleshooting, warranty, loyalty program, and escalation procedures.

## 1. Company Overview

TechNest is an electronics and home-goods retailer operating across Egypt, with expanding delivery coverage into Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Kuwait. TechNest sells through its mobile app, website, and three flagship retail locations in Cairo, Alexandria, and Giza. This handbook is the canonical reference for support agents and the AI support assistant; when a customer's situation is not covered here, escalate to a human supervisor rather than guessing.

### Support Channels

Customers can reach support via the in-app chat, the support email (support@technest-example.com), and the call center, open daily from 9 AM to 11 PM Cairo time. Live chat has the fastest average response time (under 2 minutes), followed by phone (under 5 minutes), followed by email (within 24 hours).

### Service Level Targets

Agents are expected to resolve simple account and order status questions within a single interaction. Issues requiring a refund, warranty claim, or courier investigation may take up to 5 business days to fully resolve, and the customer should be told this expectation up front.

## 2. Shipping & Delivery

### Domestic Shipping

Standard shipping within Egypt takes 3-5 business days and costs 60 EGP, waived on orders over 1,500 EGP. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs an additional 75 EGP on top of any standard fee. Orders placed before 2 PM Cairo time ship the same day; orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day. Fridays and public holidays are not counted as business days.

### International Shipping

International delivery is currently supported to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Kuwait only. Delivery takes 7-14 business days. Customs duties and import fees, where applicable, are the responsibility of the customer and are never included in the displayed order total. TechNest cannot expedite customs clearance.

### Tracking an Order

Customers receive a tracking number by SMS and email at the moment of shipment. If a customer says they never received a tracking number, ask them to check spam first; if it still cannot be found, look up the order by order ID and read the tracking number to them directly rather than resending the automated message.

### Delayed or Lost Packages

If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, open an investigation ticket with the courier using the order ID. TechNest resolves confirmed-lost packages by either reshipping the order at no cost or issuing a full refund within 3 business days of the courier confirming the loss. Do not promise a specific resolution before the courier confirms the package is lost.

### Address Changes

An address can be changed only before the order has shipped. Once a shipping label has been generated, the address is locked and the customer must either accept delivery to the original address or refuse delivery and place a new order.

## 3. Returns & Refunds

### Return Eligibility

Items can be returned within 14 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Electronics with opened packaging can only be returned if defective; a defect must be confirmed by a support agent or technician before a return label is issued. Personal care items, opened software licenses, and gift cards are non-returnable under any circumstances.

### Return Shipping Cost

Return shipping is free when the return reason is a confirmed defect, wrong item shipped, or item damaged in transit. For all other return reasons (changed mind, wrong size, etc.), the customer covers return shipping, which is deducted from the refund amount.

### Refund Processing

Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes warehouse inspection, not from the date the return was requested. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or TechNest wallet credit, chosen by the customer.

### Exchanges

TechNest does not process direct exchanges. A size, color, or model swap is handled as a standard return plus a new, separate order, because inventory availability is checked independently at the time each transaction is placed.

## 4. Payment & Billing

### Accepted Payment Methods

TechNest accepts Visa, Mastercard, Fawry, Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and cash on delivery (COD). COD is capped at 5,000 EGP per order and is not available for orders containing pre-order or custom-build items.

### Failed or Duplicate Payments

If a payment fails but the amount was still deducted from the customer's account, the reversal is automatic and handled by the issuing bank, typically within 3-10 business days. TechNest does not hold or control these funds, so agents should not promise a specific refund date for a bank-side reversal, only explain the typical timeline.

### Invoices

A tax invoice is generated and emailed automatically once payment succeeds. A company-name invoice requires the company's tax details to be entered in checkout before the order is placed; invoices cannot be reissued with different billing details after the fact.

### Installment Plans

Installment payment (2, 4, or 6 months) is available on orders over 3,000 EGP through participating banks, shown as an option at checkout. Installment eligibility is determined by the customer's bank, not by TechNest, so support agents cannot approve or override a declined installment application.

## 5. Account Management

### Password Resets

Passwords are reset from the login screen via 'Forgot Password.' The reset link expires 30 minutes after it is sent. If no email arrives within 5 minutes, ask the customer to check spam and confirm the email on file is correct before triggering a second reset.

### Merging Duplicate Accounts

A customer who registered once by phone number and again by email can have the two accounts merged by support. Order history and loyalty points move to the primary account the customer designates; the duplicate account is deactivated and cannot be reactivated afterward.

### Account Deletion

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days per the data retention policy. Any active or in-transit orders must be completed or cancelled first. Once the 30-day window closes, the account and its order history cannot be recovered, so agents must clearly warn the customer before confirming a deletion request.

## 6. Technical Troubleshooting

### App Crashes on Launch

First confirm the app is updated to the latest version. Next, clear the app cache (on Android: Settings > Apps > TechNest > Storage > Clear Cache) rather than clearing 'Data,' which would delete saved items and login state. Reinstalling the app is the last resort if the crash persists after a cache clear.

### Payment Page Not Loading

This is usually caused by an unstable connection or an outdated in-app browser. Recommend switching to Wi-Fi (or a different mobile network) and completing checkout in the phone's default browser instead of the in-app webview.

### Order Missing After Successful Payment

If the customer received a payment confirmation (SMS or email) but the order is not visible under 'My Orders,' this is typically a sync delay of up to 15 minutes. If the order still is not visible after that window, escalate with the payment reference number rather than asking the customer to retry payment.

## 7. Warranty & Repairs

### Standard Coverage

All electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Accidental damage, liquid damage, and unauthorized third-party repairs void the warranty immediately, even if the original defect was unrelated.

### Extended Warranty

An optional 12-month warranty extension can be purchased at checkout for select product categories (laptops, phones, and major appliances), extending total coverage to 24 months from the original purchase date.

### Filing a Warranty Claim

The customer submits the order ID and a description of the fault through the app or support email. Approved claims are picked up by courier for inspection at no cost to the customer. Repair or replacement typically takes 10-15 business days from the day the item is received at the service center.

## 8. TechNest Plus Loyalty Program

TechNest Plus is a free loyalty program. Members earn 1 point per 10 EGP spent, and points can be redeemed at checkout at a rate of 100 points = 10 EGP. Points expire 12 months after being earned if unused.

### Membership Tiers

Bronze (0+ points earned per year) gives standard earning rates. Silver (2,000+ points per year) adds free standard shipping on all orders. Gold (5,000+ points per year) adds free express shipping and early access to sales events.

## 9. Escalation Procedures

Escalate to a shift supervisor immediately if a customer requests legal action, threatens to post publicly about a safety issue, or reports a payment made but not reflected anywhere in the order system after 24 hours. For all other disputes, agents should attempt resolution using this handbook before escalating.

### Complaint Logging

Every complaint, resolved or not, must be logged with the order ID (if applicable), a short description, and the resolution or escalation reason, so that recurring issues can be identified across customers.
